# Lab Plastid Genome Answers — Ginkgo biloba (MN443423.1)

1. **Scientific Name & Accession Number:**
   * Scientific Name: *Ginkgo biloba* L.
   * Accession Number: MN443423.1

2. **Total Genome Length:**
   * 156,990 base pairs (bp)

3. **Region Identification (LSC, SSC, IRa, IRb):**
   * **LSC (Large Single-Copy):** The large outer arc comprising 88,923 bp.
   * **SSC (Small Single-Copy):** The smaller single-copy arc comprising 18,261 bp.
   * **IRa & IRb (Inverted Repeats):** The two identical segments separating LSC and SSC, each measuring 24,903 bp.

4. **Three Genes in LSC Region:**
   * *psbA*, *rbcL*, and *atpB*

5. **Gene in SSC Region:**
   * *ndhF* (or *ycf1*, *rps15*)

6. **Gene in Inverted Repeat Region:**
   * *rrn16* (or *rrn23*, *rps7*). Yes, these genes are shown twice on the circular map because they reside within the duplicated IRa and IRb segments.

7. **Photosynthesis-Related Gene & Function:**
   * **Gene:** *psbA*
   * **Function:** Encodes the D1 reaction center protein of Photosystem II, essential for light absorption and water oxidation during photosynthesis.

8. **Direction of Gene Arrows / Orientation:**
   * Arrows pointing clockwise indicate transcription on the outer strand, while counter-clockwise arrows indicate transcription on the inner strand.

9. **GC Content Graph:**
   * GC content is non-uniform across the genome. It is noticeably higher in the IR regions (around 43%) compared to the LSC (~37%) and SSC (~34%) regions, primarily due to high GC density in rRNA genes (*rrn*).

10. **Utility of Graphical Map vs. Raw Sequence:**
    * A circular graphical map provides an intuitive visual overview of genome architecture, gene clustering, boundaries between structural regions (LSC/SSC/IR), transcription orientation, and GC variation, which are difficult to extract from raw text or long tables.
