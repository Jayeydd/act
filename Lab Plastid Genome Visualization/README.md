## Structural Description
The *Ginkgo biloba* plastid genome displays a quadripartite circular architecture measuring 156,990 bp in total length. It contains a Large Single-Copy (LSC) region of 88,923 bp, a Small Single-Copy (SSC) region of 18,261 bp, and two identical Inverted Repeat (IRa/IRb) regions of 24,903 bp each. The GC content averages 39.56% overall but peaks sharply in the IR regions due to the high GC content of the ribosomal RNA genes (*rrn16*, *rrn23*, *rrn4.5*, *rrn5*).
